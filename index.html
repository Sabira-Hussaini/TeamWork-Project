About WFE

Education, Empowerment & Hope

Who We Are

WFE (Women for Education) is a non-profit organization dedicated to empowering Afghan girls through education, skills development, and meaningful support.

Our Mission

To provide accessible and quality learning opportunities for Afghan girls who are deprived of formal education.

Our Vision

A future where every Afghan girl has the freedom, confidence, and opportunity to learn, grow, and lead.

Why We Exist

Millions of Afghan girls are denied access to education. WFE exists to keep education alive as a source of hope and empowerment for those who need it most.
